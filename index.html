How does life adapt,
persist and recover?

Exploring the interplay of ecology and evolution in a changing world.

We study how species interactions, individual variation and adaptation shape the resilience of living systems. Our work brings together mathematical models, empirical data, microcosm experiments and fieldwork in India.
