I joined the EEDR lab as a Research Assistant in August 2026, straight after finishing my Master's thesis at the Centre for Ecological Sciences, IISc, under Prof. Vishwesha Guttal. My background is in physics — I did my BSc at Azim Premji University and my MSc at St Joseph's University, Bengaluru — and my thesis work was on rate-induced tipping points, using stochastic differential equations to understand how the speed of an external driver, not just its strength, can push a system past a point of no return. In the lab, I'm now building on Sarah Antl's work on stage-structured Lotka-Volterra competition, extending the two-species framework to communities of more than two competing species, to explore how cross-stage self-limitation and trait variation shape coexistence as community complexity grows.
Outside of work, I swim, play badminton and Crochet.
